How Data Sync Works

  • Pull from Google Sheet: Downloads store items from your Google Sheet and saves them to local Excel.
  • Export to Excel: Downloads your local store items as an Excel file for backup.
  • Import from File: Upload an Excel or CSV file to replace your local store inventory.
  • Push (Export CSV): Export your local data as CSV - you can then manually upload to Google Sheets.
