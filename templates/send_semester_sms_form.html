About This Feature

This feature allows you to send results for a specific semester to all parents via SMS. The SMS will include:

  • Student name and department
  • All subject scores for the selected semester
  • Login information for parents to view results online
Note: This will send SMS to ALL parents whose students have results for the selected semester. Please ensure you have sufficient SMS credits before proceeding.
