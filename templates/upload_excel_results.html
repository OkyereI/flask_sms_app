2. Upload Completed Results

Excel File Format Requirements

  • Required Columns: Student ID, Student Name, Student Department
  • Optional Column: Parent Phone (can be left blank)
  • Score Columns: Must follow the format: Subject Score Type - Year Semester
  • Example: Math Total Score - 2025 Semester 1
  • Supported Semesters: Semester 1, Semester 2, Sem 1, Sem 2 (with any year)
Expected Column Format:
Student ID | Student Name | Student Department | Parent Phone | Math Total Score - 2025 Semester 1 | English Grade - 2025 Semester 1 | ...

Also supports Semester 2:
Math Total Score - 2025 Semester 2 | Science Grade - 2025 Semester 2 | ...
Important: The semester is automatically detected from column headers. Ensure headers follow the format: "Subject Score Type - Year Semester" (e.g., "Math Total Score - 2025 Semester 2").
